# 16 - Combinations

| Folder | Exercises | What you practise |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| [CommonPairs](CommonPairs/README.md) | 24 | The two- and three-operator chains that make up most real queries, and the shortcuts LINQ provides for them |
| [DeferredExecution](DeferredExecution/README.md) | 8 | When queries run, how often, and what that means for captured variables, mutation and exceptions |
| [QuerySyntax](QuerySyntax/README.md) | 12 | `from / where / orderby / group / join / let / into` and how each maps to methods |
| [Capstone](Capstone/README.md) | 18 | Reports and algorithmic puzzles that need four or five operators at once |

Suggested order: CommonPairs, DeferredExecution, QuerySyntax, Capstone. Do the capstone twice - once
now, once a week later from a fresh `TODO`.
