# 02 - Projection

Turn each element into something else.

| Method | Exercises | One-liner |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| [Select](Select/README.md) | 10 | One in, one out |
| [SelectMany](SelectMany/README.md) | 10 | One in, many out - flattened |
| [Zip](Zip/README.md) | 7 | Combine two or three sequences position by position |

`Select` and `SelectMany` are the two shapes of "map"; knowing which one you need is the whole skill.
